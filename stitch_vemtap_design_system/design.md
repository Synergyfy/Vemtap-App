DESIGN THE FIRST 3 SCREENS OF THE VEMTAP NATIVE MOBILE APP.
IMPORTANT:
This is the beginning of a complete native mobile application redesign for VEMTAP.
Design ONLY the first 3 screens in this prompt.
Do not design the location screens yet.
Do not design the customer home screen yet.
Do not design the business onboarding screens yet.
Do not add bottom navigation yet.
These 3 screens are the first-time experience a user sees immediately after downloading and opening VEMTAP.
==================================================
PRODUCT
==================================================
App name:
VEMTAP
Tagline:
"One Tap. Lifetime Customer."
VEMTAP is a local discovery and customer engagement platform.
For consumers, VEMTAP helps people discover:
- deals
- businesses
- products
- services
- opportunities around their location
The core consumer experience is:
"Discover deals near you."
VEMTAP is consumer-first.
IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE:
Every person enters VEMTAP naturally as a consumer/customer.
Do NOT create a "Customer vs Business" choice.
Business owners are also customers because they patronize other businesses.
The normal VEMTAP experience should therefore always feel like a consumer discovery application.
Business functionality is a secondary path that can be accessed later through a subtle:
"Own a business? Set up your business on VEMTAP"
Do NOT make Customer and Business equal onboarding options.

==================================================

Top area:
Display the VEMTAP brand name cleanly.
Do NOT invent or generate a replacement logo.

Main visual:
Create a premium visual representing local discovery and shopping.

- a smartphone
- nearby businesses
- deal cards
- location indicators
- products
- discovery
The visual should feel like VEMTAP.
Do not use a generic stock-photo aesthetic.
Do not create a complicated illustration.
Keep it clean and modern.

"Discover More. Buy Smarter."
Supporting text:
"Find great deals, products and businesses around you — all in one place."

"Get Started"
Secondary text:
"Already have an account? Sign In"
